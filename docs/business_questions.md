# Business Questions

This document explains the business purpose behind each SQL query in the project.

## Revenue Analysis

1. **Monthly Revenue**  
   Helps understand long-term revenue growth and seasonality.

2. **Weekly Revenue**  
   Useful for short-term performance tracking.

3. **Daily Order Count**  
   Helps identify spikes, slow days, and operational load.

4. **Revenue by Weekday**  
   Helps plan promotions and staffing based on high-performing days.

5. **Revenue by Hour**  
   Helps understand customer purchase timing and peak traffic periods.

## Product and Category Analysis

6. **Top 10 Products by Revenue**  
   Identifies best-selling products.

7. **Top Product Per Category**  
   Helps understand category-level product leaders.

8. **Category Revenue Share by Month**  
   Shows which categories contribute most to monthly revenue.

## Customer Analysis

9. **Top Customer Per Month**  
   Identifies high-value customers for retention and loyalty efforts.

10. **Previous Order Date Per Customer**  
    Helps understand purchase history.

11. **Days Between Customer Purchases**  
    Supports retention and reactivation analysis.

12. **Repeat Customer Percentage**  
    Measures customer loyalty.

13. **Monthly Unique Customers**  
    Tracks active purchasing customers.

14. **New vs Returning Customers**  
    Helps compare acquisition and retention performance.

15. **Customer Lifetime Value Ranking**  
    Identifies the most valuable customers.

## Payment Analysis

16. **Payment Status Percentage by Month**  
    Tracks successful, pending, and failed payment distribution.

17. **Payment Failure Month-over-Month Trend**  
    Helps detect payment reliability issues.

## Growth Analysis

18. **Running Monthly Revenue**  
    Shows cumulative revenue growth.

19. **Month-over-Month Revenue Growth**  
    Shows whether business performance is improving or declining.

20. **Average Order Value by Month**  
    Tracks customer spending behavior over time.
